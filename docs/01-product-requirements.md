# Virtual Cafe — Product Requirements

## 1. Product Overview

Virtual Cafe is a web application that lets users create an immersive cafe-like environment through interactive virtual scenes and ambient sounds, enabling them to find study groups and accountability partners online. 

## 2. Problem Statement

Students and working professionals who work/study from home may find it hard to concentrate and be productive in isolation. Virtual cafe concept is the AI era idea which helps people with similar interests or goals work/study together and increase their productivity.

## 3. Target Users

- Students studying for competitive exams
- Remote workers
- People who enjoy ambient environments while working or relaxing

## 4. Core Features

### Existing Features

- Multiple virtual scenes
- Customizable ambient sound mixing
- Chat rooms and study groups
- Focused session timer (Pomodoro)
